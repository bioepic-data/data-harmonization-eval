## Decision: INCLUDE

sensors. Rules 1, 3, 4, 5 do not trigger exclusion.

## Payload identification

Files staged under `inputs/raw/<id>/`:
- `BM_Merged_T_VWC_0616_1018.csv` — **measurement payload**. Datalogger output
  from 7 CS655 sensors: columns `date.time`, `record`, `Volumetric Water
  Content`, `Electrical Conductivity (dS/m)`, `T (degrees celcius)`,
  `Depth (cm)`. 56,861 hourly rows, Jun 2016 – Oct 2018, 7 depths
  (10, 20, 30, 50, 75, 100, 130 cm).
- `BM_EGM_Well_CO2.csv` — pCO2 (soil gas) measurements, NOT soil moisture, so
  not harmonized to the target schema. It is used only as a **location source**
  (columns `Location`, `Latitude`, `Longitude`).

The package metadata lists a third file, `BM_EGM_Surface_Flux.csv` (CO2 surface
flux), but it is **not present** in the staged inputs. It is not a soil moisture
payload and its absence does not affect the harmonization. Recorded here as a
limitation rather than retrieved externally.

## Time series determination

`is_timeseries = True`. Hourly datalogger output from fixed sensors deployed
Jun 2016; many timestamps per site+depth.

## Timezone

The package does not state a timezone. The site is in Gothic, CO. Consistent
with other East River, Colorado datasets in this project, local time is treated
as fixed MST (`Etc/GMT+7`, no DST) when converting to UTC. This is an
assumption; a ±1 h shift during DST periods is possible if the logger actually
recorded MDT.

`spatialCoverage` record (both NW/SE corners identical). `qc_flag` left empty
(coordinates resolved from supplied package data; not g1/g2).
