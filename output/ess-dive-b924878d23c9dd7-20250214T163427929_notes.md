## Payload identification
Only one payload file was staged and it is the measurement table. It is also the
source of per-row location coordinates, so it serves as both the data payload and
the location metadata file.

## Inclusion decision — INCLUDE
Rule 2 (measurement type): the column `Soil Water Content (g H2O per gram soil)`
is a direct observation of **gravimetric water content** (48 hr oven-dry per the
package `measurementTechnique`). Rule 4 (payload): a parseable dated table exists.
Rule 5 (metadata): per-row coordinates and traceable site identifiers are present.

Rule 3 (experimental manipulation) — FLAGGED but INCLUDED: the package describes
soil cores collected "at the locations of the early snowmelt-timing manipulation
experiments." However, the water-content measurements are destructive oven-dry
gravimetric measurements on field-collected cores, and the payload does not
distinguish a manipulation treatment vs. ambient control column. The measurements
reflect ambient soil moisture at the sampling footprints rather than an imposed
microclimate signal, so they were included. This differs from the excluded
index-20 warming-plot dataset, where observations directly reflected the imposed
manipulation. See open question below.

## Depth handling (qc_flag d1)
`Depth_Increment` reports ranges, so every depth is approximated (qc_flag `d1`):
- `0-5 cm`  -> 0.025 m (midpoint)
- `5-15 cm` -> 0.10 m (midpoint)
- `15-May`  -> 0.10 m. This is an Excel auto-date corruption of "5-15"; it occurs
  only on the later campaigns (2017) in the same slot where earlier campaigns
  report `5-15 cm`, confirming the intended value.
- `15 cm +` -> 0.15 m (open-ended lower bound; no upper bound reported).

Note on schema: the harmonized CSV schema (SECTION 1) has no per-row depth-QC
column, so the `d1` depth-approximation flag is documented here and in the change
mapping. The `qc_flag` column required by AGENT_INSTRUCTIONS lives in the
`_location.csv` file and encodes coordinate resolution (`g2`).

## Time series determination
`is_timeseries = False`. Sampling occurred on nine discrete campaign dates
(2016-09-12, 2016-10-20, 2017-03-07, 2017-05-09, 2017-06-09, 2017-07-24,
2017-08-01, 2017-09-15, 2017-10-27). Each site+depth has at most one destructive
core measurement per campaign; these are campaign dates, not continuous logger
output.

## site_id construction
No single site-code column exists. `site_id` is built as
`Location|Field_Site|Block|Plot|Topographic_Position`. Topographic_Position
(Upslope/Downslope, and the snow-pit labels) is kept because it separates distinct
sampling footprints with distinct coordinates within a plot. This yields 59
distinct sites across the harmonized rows.

## Location resolution (qc_flag g2)
Priority 1 of SECTION 5 applies: coordinates are in the payload itself. For each
site_id, latitude/longitude are the mean of the non-null per-row coordinates.
- 46 sites resolved to coordinates.
- 13 sites have no coordinates in any row (all Washington_Gulch `Upper_Montane`
  plots, plus the Lower_Subalpine `Willow` and `Veratrum` plots) and are emitted
  with empty lat/lon and `qc_flag = g2`. Package-level `spatialCoverage` provides
  only a bounding box (NW 39.034/-107.05, SE 38.88/-106.88), which is not a point
  location and was not used to invent coordinates.

Per-row coordinates for a given plot vary slightly across campaigns (repeated GPS
fixes); the mean is used as the representative point. A handful of raw coordinate
cells contained a stray replacement character prefix (e.g. `\ufffd38.920...`);
these were coerced numerically with `errors="coerce"` and contribute where valid.
