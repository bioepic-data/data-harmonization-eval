# Harmonization notes — dataset index 3

- **Dataset:** `ess-dive-4c1829de1b8a2ec-20260220T045039633`
- **DOI:** `doi:10.15485/2998779`
- **Title:** Data for "Depth of nutrient uptake by deep-rooted plants is regulated by water availability" (Watershed Function SFA, East River, CO)

## Payload identification

The data package contains many files (Sr/ICPMS, isotopes, canopy size,
dendrochronology, modeling results), but only `Soil_water_potential.csv`
contains direct soil moisture observations. It is a CR1000 datalogger export
of soil matric potential. `locations.csv` is the ESS-DIVE location-metadata
ancillary file. All other files are geochemical/plant/modeling payloads
outside the target schema. `flmd.csv`, `dd.csv`, and `samples.csv` are
documentation/metadata and were not staged in `inputs/raw/`.

## Inclusion decision

**INCLUDE.** The dataset reports direct observations of soil matric
potential (water potential), one of the three accepted measurement types
(RULE 2). It is a continuous sensor deployment (RULE 4 satisfied), not a
modeled/derived product, and coordinates are resolvable from the package
ancillary file (RULE 5).

## Variable mapping

Source `Soil_water_potential.csv` is in wide format. Five matric-potential
(MP) sensor columns encode site and depth in their names:

| Source column                          | site_id | depth_m | replicate |
|-----------------------------------------|---------|---------|-----------|
| `SD11-25cm_MP`                          | SD11    | 0.25    | 1         |
| `SD11-60cm_MP_2_Avg`                    | SD11    | 0.60    | 1         |
| `SD1-25cm_MP_3_Avg`                     | SD1     | 0.25    | 1         |
| `SD1-60cm_MP_4_Avg`                     | SD1     | 0.60    | 1         |
| `SD1-25cm_sagebrush_rhizo_MP_5_Avg`     | SD1     | 0.25    | 2         |

- **water_potential_kPa:** direct kPa; no unit conversion.
- **depth_m:** parsed from column names (25 cm → 0.25 m, 60 cm → 0.60 m).
- **site_id:** parsed from column prefix (`SD1`, `SD11`).
- **replicate:** SD1 0.25 m has two co-located sensors (bulk soil = 1,
  sagebrush rhizosphere = 2); all other site/depth pairs have a single
  sensor (replicate 1).
- **VWC / GWC:** not reported; populated with NA.
- The `*_Temp_*`, `BattV_*`, `PTemp_C_Avg`, and `RECORD` columns are logger
  diagnostics/temperatures and are not part of the target schema; dropped.

### Depth discrepancy (flagged)
The package abstract states soil water potential was collected "at 2 depths
(30 cm and 60 cm)", but the measurement column names say **25 cm** and 60 cm.
Column names were treated as authoritative (0.25 m and 0.60 m). The 30 cm
figure in the prose may be approximate; no `d1` flag was applied because the
depths are given explicitly in the data columns rather than from a range.

## Time series determination

`is_timeseries = True`. The file is a 10-minute-interval datalogger export
with ~27,953 timestamped records spanning 2024-11-09 to 2025-06-04,
consistent with the abstract ("continuous collection ... from Nov 24 –
Jun 25").

## Timestamp handling

`TIMESTAMP` is logger local time. The East River site is Mountain Time;
dataloggers are typically set to Mountain Standard Time (no DST). Parsed with
`%Y-%m-%d %H:%M` in `Etc/GMT+7` and converted to UTC, consistent with the
East River exemplars (datasets 23, 25, 26).

## Sentinel / QA handling

The MPS-6 / TEROS21 matric-potential sensors report logger error codes
(`NAN`, `INF`) and a dryness overrange. The value distribution is strongly
bimodal: valid field readings span about -0.1 to -1000 kPa (a thin tail to
~-2100 kPa), then an **empty band between -3000 and -6000 kPa**, then a dense
overrange cluster from -6000 to -7999 kPa (including a repeated `-7999`
sentinel). All error codes, non-finite values, and readings at or below
**-3000 kPa** were set to missing. The -3000 kPa cutoff sits inside the empty
band, so no legitimate reading is discarded. After filtering, water potential
ranges from -0.1 to -2094 kPa.

## Location resolution

Priority order (SECTION 5): `locations.csv` is a package ancillary file
(priority 2). It contains a single location record — `ER-LLN1`, "North Facing
Hillslope Sagebrush Site 1" — at **lat 38.920555, lon -106.948333**. The
sensor site labels `SD1` and `SD11` are the two sampling points described in
the abstract as being at this one north-facing hillslope sagebrush site, so
the single reported coordinate was applied to both `site_id` values.
`qc_flag` is empty (coordinates resolved from the package itself, not from an
external registry). The package-level ESS-DIVE metadata (bounding box only)
was not needed.

## Open questions / limitations

1. **Site↔coordinate granularity.** `locations.csv` gives one coordinate for
   the whole site; SD1 and SD11 are assigned identical coordinates. If the two
   sensor profiles are at physically distinct points, per-point coordinates
   are not available in the staged inputs.
2. **Depth prose vs. column names.** Abstract says 30/60 cm; columns say
   25/60 cm. Column names used (see above).
3. **Replicate semantics at SD1 0.25 m.** The bulk-soil and sagebrush
   rhizosphere sensors were encoded as replicates 1 and 2 at the same
   site/depth; they are co-located but may represent distinct microsites.
4. **Timezone assumption.** MST (`Etc/GMT+7`, no DST) assumed for the logger
   following East River exemplars; not explicitly confirmed in staged metadata.
